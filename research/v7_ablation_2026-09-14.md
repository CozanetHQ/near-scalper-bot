## Reading

1. **V7 wins every mechanical dimension:** expectancy flips positive, maxDD
   drops 13 points, tail kills fall 6x, daily trips halve, fee burn falls
   ~4x (318 vs 1,233 trades). The corr cap + slot scarcity + NEAR demotion
   are visibly doing the work — this window's damage came in correlated
   clusters, and V7's caps sliced exactly there.
2. **Both arms bled on this window** (final balances < $10): the 30d tape was
   hostile chop for the EMA-pullback signal — consistent with the 09-12
   audit's fee-limited conclusion (Arm A's −0.0919%/trade ≈ its −0.086%).
   Disclosed NOT modeled: the live EV gate (filters low-edge entries), the
   5x/15x/20x leverage re-tiering, 4-min tick cadence, Engine 2. The live
   paper epoch remains the true out-of-sample test.
3. **NEAR demotion validated in-sample:** NEAR at 1 slot went from −$0.28
   (Arm A, 142 trades) to +$0.01 (Arm B, 34 trades) — it barely trades,
   which is the point.
