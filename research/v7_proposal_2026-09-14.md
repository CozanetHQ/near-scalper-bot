## 1. Answer first: leverage IS already automatic (owner 2026-09-13 build)

`compute_leverage_zones()` re-tiers every pair on every run from the trailing
14 days of closed trades in `data/trades.jsonl`:

| Evidence (bootstrap CI) | Tier | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Pair conclusively bad pooled (n≥30, CI hi < 0) | 5x everywhere | NEAR is already here — registry-locked at 5x in all 8 buckets |
| 3-hour bucket conclusive CI < 0 (n≥25) | 5x that bucket | Down-tier in the losing session |
| Bucket conclusive CI > 0 (n≥25) | 15x that bucket | Up-tier |
| — and mean ≥ +0.10%/trade | 20x that bucket | Strong up-tier |
| No conclusive evidence | registry tier (10x) | Young pairs stay flat |

So YES: a pair that performs well earns 15x/20x automatically, and the hard
stop scales with the tier (`HARD_SL_BY_LEV` = {5x:12%, 10x:6%, 15x:4%, 20x:3%})
so higher leverage is never a bigger tail. The evidence bar is deliberately
high — a couple of good trades cannot re-tier a pair; ~25–30 conclusive
trades can. **No V7 change needed here. It already does what the owner asked.**

## 2. V7-A — Fixed clips + balance-scaled slots

### Current sizing (for contrast)
`slot_cap = balance × 0.85 × lev / 8` → ~$10.6 notional per slot on the $10
account. The account is sliced 8 ways whether it has $3 or $300 — the owner's
complaint ("very very very small") is exactly right.

### V7-A design
- `FIXED_NOTIONAL_USD = 33.0` — every position is a $33 clip (10x → $3.3
  margin; at 15x/20x tiers the margin cost per clip DROPS to $2.20/$1.65,
  which buys headroom, not extra slots).
- `SLOT_COST_MARGIN = 3.30` — one slot "costs" $3.30 of balance.
- `N_SLOTS = max(1, floor(balance / SLOT_COST_MARGIN))` — slot count is a
  pure function of balance:

| Balance | Slots | Margin committed | vs. V5 (8 × balance-slice) |
|---|---|---|---|
| $10.00 | 3 | $9.90 (99%) | 8 × ~$10.6 cap each |
| $13.20 | 4 | $13.20 (100%) | same |
| $16.50 | 5 | $16.50 (100%) | same |
| $26.40 | 8 | $26.40 (100%) | same |
| $50.00 | 15 | $49.50 (99%) | same |

Synergy: this rule is self-sizing in drawdown — if balance falls, slots
shrink automatically; it composes with RECOVERY_SIZE_FRAC below the SAFE_DD
wall. All other rails stay wired: 90s cooldown, DAILY_LOSS_LIMIT 12%,
SAFE_DD wall, MAX_POS_AGE kill, MAE ceiling.

### Honest risk math (the part the owner must read)
1. **The same 110 trades re-run at $33 would have netted −$0.11, not +$0.53**
   (fees $4.21). Bigger clips do NOT fix the session's structure: the 4 NEAR
   kills would have cost −$5.36 vs +$5.25 of wins. Fixed $33 clips amplify the
   existing 18:1 loss/win asymmetry dollar-for-dollar. **V7 without the NEAR
   fix is roughly the same bot with louder losses.**
2. **Correlated kill scenario:** BTC/ETH/SOL correlate ~0.8–0.9. Three $33
   longs on that cluster hit by one −6% candle = 3 × −$1.98 = **−$5.94 on a
   $10 account (−59%) in five minutes.** V5's 8-slot budget only committed
   ~85%; V7-A commits ~99%. Proposal: `CORR_DIR_CAP = 2` — max 2
   same-direction positions in {BTC, ETH, SOL} at once (XRP/NEAR separate).
3. One MAE_KILL at $33 notional (avg −4.06% this session) = **−$1.34 = 13% of
   a $10 account** per kill.

## 3. V7-B — BE floor above the owner's 0.2% fee allowance

Current: `BE_BUFFER_FRAC = 0.0019` (0.12% taker RT + 0.02% slip + 0.05% crumb).
Live evidence this session: 72 BE_STOP exits, **zero net-negative**, but the
locked crumb averaged only +0.07% of notional (~$0.007/trade) — the owner is
right that it's functionally zero.

V7-B: `BE_BUFFER_FRAC = 0.0027` = owner's 0.2% fee allowance + 0.02% assumed
slip + 0.05% crumb. **Doubles the guaranteed crumb per BE exit (~+0.14% of
notional, ~$0.046 per $33 clip).**

Trade-off (stated a priori): a higher floor means some trades that would have
retraced 0.19% then run to TP will instead exit at BE — the 09-11 MFE study
(91% of 50%-of-TP reaches touch) says this converts a small share of full TPs
into BE crumbs. Both outcomes are logged by reason code, so this is directly
measurable after 100 armed trades. If BE-capture rises while TP count collapses,
0.0027 was too greedy and the follow-up lock must say so.

## 5. Validation plan (before any live-money discussion)

1. **Backtest ablation, same machinery as the 09-12 audit** (multislot_ablation
   style): Arm A = current v5 sizing, Arm B = V7 fixed clips, identical signals
   and exits, 30d × 5 pairs. Accept: EV/trade after costs ≥ Arm A, bootstrap CI
   on the difference, maxDD < 50%, correlated-cluster kill stress included.
2. **Monte Carlo ruin** (stress_mc machinery) at 3 slots × $33 on $10:
   P(ruin), median terminal, DD distribution. The 09-12 audit's discipline
   applies to our own proposal: if V7-A loses the ablation, it ships with that
   number on the label or it doesn't ship.
3. **Paper epoch ≥ 30 days under locked V7 params**, out-of-sample: pooled
   EV/trade 95% CI lower bound > 0 after costs; fee burn < gross edge;
   per-reason exit capture sanity (BE crumb ≥ +0.1% of notional).
